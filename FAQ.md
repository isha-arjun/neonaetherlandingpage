# Neon Aether — Frequently Asked Questions

> **A spoiler-free FAQ about Neon Aether.**

> Answers are based on information confirmed by the developer and the current game materials. Details that have not been officially confirmed are identified as such. This FAQ avoids major story spoilers.

## What is Neon Aether?

Neon Aether is a story-driven, 2D side-scrolling science-fiction platformer. It follows Andrew, a human who discovers a mysterious portal and is transported to an unfamiliar world. The game combines platforming, combat, exploration, characters, and a mystery-filled science-fiction story.

## What genre is Neon Aether?

Neon Aether is a 2D side-scrolling action platformer with a strong narrative and science-fiction adventure focus.

## What is the story about?

The story follows Andrew after he discovers a portal in his home and arrives on Aethelgard, a strange planet with its own ruler, society, and conflicts. As he encounters unfamiliar people and places, Andrew becomes caught up in events far beyond his ordinary life.

This is a spoiler-free overview. The deeper mysteries and major story developments are best experienced in the game.

## Who is the main character?

The protagonist is Andrew, a human from Earth. His journey begins when he discovers a mysterious portal in his home and is transported to Aethelgard.

## Who are some of the important characters?

The early story introduces Andrew, Nyx, Valtor, Solan, Tyro, Queen Vespera, and Raya. They have different roles in Aethelgard's society and in Andrew's journey. See the character guide for spoiler-free profiles.

## What is Aethelgard?

Aethelgard is the first planet Andrew visits. It is a radiant world illuminated by multiple suns, with cities featuring glass and white-stone architecture. It is ruled by Queen Vespera and governed by a strict system of order.

## What are the Novari?

The Novari are a clan of assassins hunted by Aethelgard's guards. Nyx is a member of the Novari.

## What kind of gameplay does Neon Aether have?

The game includes 2D platforming, jumping, navigating levels, close-range combat, blocking, and encounters with enemies and bosses. The story and exploration are integrated into Andrew's journey.

## Does Neon Aether have combat?

Yes. The developer-provided script includes close-range combat, blocking, punches, kicks, power moves, and blade attacks. Combat prompts also describe timing and cooldowns. Specific mechanics and balance may change during development.
