About Neon Aether

A spoiler-free overview of Neon Aether, a story-driven 2D science-fiction platformer.

A story-driven science-fiction platforming adventure.

What is Neon Aether?

Neon Aether is a 2D side-scrolling sci-fi platformer following Andrew, an ordinary human whose discovery of a mysterious portal takes him far beyond the world he knows.

The game combines platforming, combat, exploration, character encounters and narrative discovery, with a retro-inspired 16-bit pixel-art direction.

The premise

Andrew discovers a mysterious portal in his home and is transported to Aethelgard, a radiant planet illuminated by multiple suns. Its striking cities and strict system of rule make life difficult for a human outsider.

As Andrew travels through unfamiliar places, he encounters people with different loyalties and perspectives. The deeper mysteries are best discovered in the game.

Gameplay at a glance

  • 2D side-scrolling platforming and movement.
  • Close-range combat, blocking and enemy encounters.
  • Exploration of distinct science-fiction environments.
  • A character-led story built around mystery and discovery.

Platforms and release

Neon Aether is planned for release on Steam for PC and Mac. A public release date, system requirements, price and Steam store page have not been confirmed.
