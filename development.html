Development approach

Neon Aether is being developed iteratively, with playable content and the developer's game materials informing refinements to gameplay, presentation and the overall experience.

Core creative pillars

  • Story and discovery: A mystery-led science-fiction journey told through Andrew's experiences.
  • Platforming and action: Side-scrolling movement, jumping, close-range combat and enemy encounters.
  • Atmosphere: Distinct environments and a retro-inspired 16-bit pixel-art identity.
  • Characters: Encounters with people shaped by different roles, loyalties and perspectives.

Current status

The game is in active development, and playable levels exist. Features and implementation details may evolve as development continues.

Platforms

Neon Aether is planned for Windows and macOS through Steam, with separate releases planned for the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. The current target launch date shown on the homepage is February 10, 2027; it is a target, not a guaranteed or confirmed release date. The developer-confirmed planned price is US$5.99. Final regional pricing may vary. System requirements and store pages have not been confirmed.
