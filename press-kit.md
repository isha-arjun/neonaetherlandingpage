## Short description

**Neon Aether** is a story-driven 2D sci-fi platformer following Andrew, an ordinary human whose discovery of a mysterious portal takes him to unfamiliar worlds. Explore atmospheric environments, encounter unusual characters, navigate platforming challenges, and face enemies in a mystery-filled science-fiction adventure.

## Long description

Andrew's life changes when he discovers a mysterious portal in his home and is transported to Aethelgard, a radiant planet illuminated by multiple suns. Its striking cities and strict system of rule hide a society full of tension—and Andrew, as a human outsider, quickly finds himself in danger.

Neon Aether is a 2D side-scrolling science-fiction platformer that combines platforming, close-range combat, exploration, and narrative discovery. As Andrew travels through unfamiliar places, he encounters characters with different loyalties and perspectives and becomes caught up in conflicts beyond his everyday experience.

With a retro-inspired 16-bit pixel-art identity and atmospheric science-fiction environments, Neon Aether invites players to discover its universe through the journey itself. Its major mysteries and story twists are best experienced in the game.

## Story overview

The story begins when Andrew discovers a portal in his home and arrives on Aethelgard. The planet is ruled by Queen Vespera, and its authorities regard humans as dangerous outsiders.

Andrew's journey brings him into contact with Nyx, a member of the hunted Novari clan, and other characters connected to Aethelgard's society and conflicts.
