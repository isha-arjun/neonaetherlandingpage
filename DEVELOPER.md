## About Neon Aether

*Neon Aether* is a story-driven 2D side-scrolling science-fiction action platformer. The game follows Andrew, a human protagonist whose discovery of a mysterious portal takes him from Earth to unfamiliar worlds.

The project combines:

- Narrative-led science fiction and mystery.
- Original characters, locations, and worldbuilding.
- 2D side-scrolling platforming and close-range combat.
- Exploration and atmospheric environments.
- A retro-inspired 16-bit pixel-art visual identity.

The game is being developed with an emphasis on connecting gameplay to storytelling and giving its fictional worlds a distinct identity. It is in active development, with playable levels.
