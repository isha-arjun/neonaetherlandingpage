# Neon Aether — Development

## About the project

Neon Aether is an indie, story-driven 2D side-scrolling science-fiction platformer. It is being developed around a central goal: bringing together engaging platforming, action, atmospheric worlds, memorable characters, and a mystery-led narrative.

The project is in active development, and playable levels exist.

## Creative vision

The creative direction of Neon Aether brings together four central elements:

### A story worth discovering

The game is built around Andrew's journey through unfamiliar worlds. The narrative introduces new places, characters, conflicts, and mysteries as the player progresses.

The story is intended to reward curiosity and discovery. Major plot developments are deliberately kept private so players can experience them in the game.

### Distinct science-fiction worlds

Neon Aether's universe features different planets and societies, each with its own atmosphere, environments, and sources of conflict.

Aethelgard, the first world encountered, contrasts radiant architecture and bright planetary skies with a strict system of authority and tension beneath the surface.

### Platforming and action

The gameplay combines side-scrolling platforming with close-range combat and enemy encounters. The developer's game script documents movement, jumping, blocking, attack sequences, and boss encounters.

### A retro-inspired visual identity

Neon Aether uses a 16-bit pixel-art direction combined with science-fiction environments and atmospheric presentation. The aim is to give the game a recognizable visual identity while supporting the mood of its different locations.

## Current development status

**Confirmed status:** Neon Aether is in active development, with playable levels.

The developer-provided materials document:
- A story and cast centred on Andrew's journey.
- Multiple named locations within Aethelgard.
- Platforming and movement controls.
- Close-range combat instructions, including blocking and attack sequences.
- Enemy and boss encounters.
- A wider science-fiction setting beyond the first world.

This page does not assign completion percentages or publish a final content count because those figures are not confirmed here.

## Design priorities

The project's documented direction places emphasis on:

- Connecting gameplay with the story.
- Giving environments and locations distinct identities.
- Introducing characters with different roles and perspectives.
- Combining platforming challenges with combat encounters.
- Maintaining a consistent retro-inspired science-fiction art direction.
- Preserving important mysteries for players to discover.

These are creative priorities, not a guarantee that every planned idea is already implemented.

## How the game is being developed

Neon Aether is being developed iteratively. Playable content and the developer's game materials provide the basis for refining gameplay, presentation, and the overall experience.
