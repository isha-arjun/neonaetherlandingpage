## What is Neon Aether?

Neon Aether is an indie science-fiction game built around a story of discovery, unfamiliar worlds, and the people who inhabit them.

The adventure begins when Andrew discovers a mysterious portal in his home. He is transported to Aethelgard, a radiant planet with multiple suns, striking architecture, and a strict system of rule. As a human outsider, Andrew finds himself in a world where his presence is not welcomed by the authorities.

The game invites players to explore this unfamiliar universe through Andrew's journey. Its story is designed to unfold through the places he visits, the characters he meets, and the conflicts he encounters. The major mysteries and twists are intentionally not revealed in this overview.

## Gameplay

Neon Aether combines several core gameplay elements:

- **Platforming:** Move through side-scrolling levels, jump across obstacles, and navigate dangerous environments.
- **Combat:** Face hostile opponents using close-range attacks, blocking, and other documented combat moves.
- **Enemy encounters:** Deal with guards and other opponents encountered during the journey.
- **Boss encounters:** The developer's game script includes larger boss encounters.
- **Exploration:** Discover unfamiliar places and meet characters who help shape the journey.
- **Narrative progression:** Gameplay encounters and environments are part of Andrew's unfolding story.

The developer-provided script documents keyboard controls including WASD for movement, Space for jumping, and Shift for blocking. Controls and gameplay details may evolve during development.

## Story and setting

The story begins on Earth and moves to Aethelgard, the first world Andrew encounters.

Aethelgard is illuminated by multiple suns and features radiant cities built with glass and white stone. It is ruled by Queen Vespera, whose authorities enforce a strict system of order. The planet's guards regard humans as dangerous outsiders.

Andrew's journey brings him into contact with people from different sides of Aethelgard's society, including Nyx, a member of the hunted Novari clan. The world contains its own political tensions, locations, and mysteries.

Neon Aether's wider science-fiction universe includes distinct worlds and societies. This overview deliberately avoids details that would reveal later story developments.

## Characters

The early story introduces a cast with different roles in Aethelgard:

- **Andrew:** The human protagonist from Earth.
- **Nyx:** A resourceful and irreverent member of the Novari.

- **Solan:** An observant guide with personal reasons to oppose Queen Vespera.
- **Tyro:** A telepath who offers help to the group.
