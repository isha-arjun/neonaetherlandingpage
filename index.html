01 / The journey begins

A doorway into the unknown.

Andrew is an ordinary human from Earth. When he discovers a mysterious portal in his home, he is transported to Aethelgard — a radiant planet illuminated by multiple suns, with striking architecture and a strict system of rule.

As an outsider in an unfamiliar society, Andrew must navigate new places, dangerous encounters, and the people who shape his journey. The deeper mysteries are best discovered in the game.

04 / How it plays

Move. Fight. Discover.
