Characters

Meet the characters of Neon Aether in this spoiler-free character guide.

A spoiler-free introduction to the people Andrew encounters.

Andrew

Andrew is the human protagonist. His journey begins when he discovers a mysterious portal in his home and arrives in Aethelgard. He is curious, outspoken and often responds to unfamiliar situations with humour.

Nyx

Nyx is a member of the Novari, a clan of assassins hunted by Aethelgard's guards. She is resourceful, irreverent and willing to take risks.

Valtor

Valtor is a high-ranking military commander who pursues the human in the name of maintaining order. He is stoic and methodical.

Solan

Solan is an observant guide who knows a route toward the Royal Palace and has a personal grievance against the Queen.

Tyro

Tyro is a telepath who can perceive other people's thoughts and offers help to the group.

Raya

Raya is a mountain sentry and one of the Queen's protectors. Raya patrols the high peaks and challenges those who cross the guarded perimeter.

Queen Vespera

Queen Vespera rules Aethelgard. Her government maintains a strict system of order.

These profiles intentionally avoid hidden identities, character fates and later story revelations.
