NEON AETHER / PROJECT ARCHIVE

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Neon Aether, its gameplay, platforms and development.

What is Neon Aether?

Neon Aether is a story-driven 2D side-scrolling science-fiction action platformer following Andrew, a human who discovers a mysterious portal and arrives in an unfamiliar world.

What kind of game is it?

It combines platforming, close-range combat, exploration, character encounters and narrative discovery.

Which platforms are planned?

Neon Aether is planned for release on Steam for PC and Mac.

When will it release?

A public release date has not been confirmed. Check the official website and official social channels for announcements.

How much will it cost?

The public price has not been confirmed.

Is there a Steam page?

A public Steam store page has not been confirmed. Do not rely on unofficial listings or assume a purchase link exists.

Does the game include combat?

Yes. The developer-provided gameplay materials include close-range combat, blocking, punches, kicks, power moves and blade attacks. Specific mechanics and balance may change during development.

Will the game contain story twists?

Neon Aether is built around mystery and narrative discovery. This website keeps major twists and later story revelations out of public descriptions.

How can I follow the project?

Visit the official website and join the waitlist for updates.
