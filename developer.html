Developer

Meet the independent developer behind Neon Aether.

About the developer

Neon Aether is an independent game project led by its creator, who guides the creative direction and development.

The project brings together game design, science-fiction storytelling, pixel art, worldbuilding and interactive experiences.

Creative direction

The goal is to build a story-driven 2D platformer with memorable characters, atmospheric environments, action and mysteries that players can discover for themselves.

Follow the project
