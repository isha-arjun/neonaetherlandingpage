## How to read this glossary

Each entry may include:

- **Category:** The type of term.
- **Aliases:** Alternate names only where supported by the available source material. An em dash means no confirmed alias is recorded.
- **Pronunciation:** A suggested reading, marked *provisional* until confirmed by the developer. These are practical English-language guides, not official phonetic canon.
- **Definition:** A concise, spoiler-free explanation.
- **Related terms:** Other entries that provide useful context.

Location labels are distinguished from confirmed proper names. Capitalization follows the terminology selected for this glossary and the available game materials.

## A–Z glossary

### Characters and titles

#### Andrew
- **Category:** Character; protagonist
- **Aliases:** —
- **Pronunciation:** AN-droo (*provisional*)
- **Definition:** The human protagonist from Earth. Andrew discovers a mysterious portal in his home and is transported to Aethelgard, where he encounters unfamiliar places, people, and conflicts.
- **Related terms:** Earth; Aethelgard; Portal

#### Nyx
- **Category:** Character; Novari
- **Aliases:** —
- **Pronunciation:** NIKS (*provisional*)
- **Definition:** A Novari assassin and rebel. Nyx becomes an important companion during Andrew's journey through Aethelgard.
- **Related terms:** The Novari; The Royal Guards; Andrew

#### Queen Vespera
- **Category:** Character; ruler; political title
- **Aliases:** Vespera (short form used in prose)
- **Pronunciation:** ves-PAIR-uh (*provisional*)
- **Definition:** The ruler of Aethelgard. Her authority shapes the planet's system of rule and the way its authorities treat humans and the Novari.
- **Related terms:** Aethelgard; Aethelgard's royal government; The Royal Palace; Queen's protectors

#### Raya
- **Category:** Character; mountain sentry; royal protector
- **Aliases:** —
- **Pronunciation:** RAY-uh (*provisional*)
- **Definition:** A mountain sentry and one of Queen Vespera's protectors. Raya patrols the high mountain region near the Royal Palace and guards the approach across a guarded perimeter.
- **Related terms:** Queen Vespera; Queen's protectors; The Mountain Border; The Royal Palace

#### Solan
- **Category:** Character; guide; ally
- **Aliases:** —
- **Pronunciation:** SOH-lan (*provisional*)
- **Definition:** An observant guide and ally who helps Andrew and his companions navigate their route toward the Royal Palace. The available character guide notes that Solan has personal reasons to oppose Queen Vespera; further details are intentionally not included here.
- **Related terms:** Andrew; Nyx; Queen Vespera; The Royal Palace

#### Tyro
- **Category:** Character; telepath; ally
- **Aliases:** —
- **Pronunciation:** TIE-roh (*provisional*)
- **Definition:** A telepath who offers help to the group. Tyro's established ability is to perceive the thoughts of others.
- **Related terms:** Telepathy; Andrew; Nyx; Solan

#### Valtor
- **Category:** Character; military commander
- **Aliases:** —
- **Pronunciation:** VAL-tor (*provisional*)
- **Definition:** A high-ranking military commander associated with Aethelgard's enforcement of order. Valtor is described as stoic and methodical.
- **Related terms:** Aethelgard's royal government; The Royal Guards; Queen Vespera

### Factions, groups, and political terms

- **Aliases:** —

## Unconfirmed terminology

The following distinctions are important when describing the setting:

- **The City** is a descriptive label, not a confirmed proper name.
- **The Prison** is a descriptive label, not a confirmed proper name.
- **The Mountain Border** describes a guarded perimeter; it is not confirmed as an official proper name.
- **The Mainland**, **The Wastes**, and **The Frontier** are treated as officially named regions, but detailed geography and history are not established in this glossary.
- **Queen's protectors** is a group/role description. Do not invent a complete roster or additional ranks.
- The exact names, designs, and technical mechanisms of individual portals, spaceships, protective equipment, and mysterious objects are not specified here.
- Exact mechanics and implementation details for respawning, level transitions, and level completion may evolve during development.

No unconfirmed story names, later-chapter identities, or speculative lore have been added as canon.

## Controls reference

The available game instruction prompt documents these keyboard controls:

| Action | Control shown in the script |
|---|---|
| Movement | WASD |
| Jumping | Space |
| Blocking | Shift |

The documented combat prompts describe click sequences:

| Combat mode | Click sequence | Move |
|---|---:|---|
| Unarmed | One click | Punch |
| Unarmed | Two clicks | Kick |
| Unarmed | Three clicks | Power move |
| Blade | One click | Stab |
| Blade | Two clicks | Slash |
| Blade | Three clicks | Power move |

Cooldowns are documented for kicks and power moves, and for certain blade attacks. Controls, combat balance, and gameplay implementation may change during development.
