# Neon Aether

> Official, spoiler-free overview of the game and project.

Neon Aether is an indie, story-driven 2D side-scrolling science-fiction action platformer with retro-inspired 16-bit pixel art. It combines platforming, combat, exploration, character encounters, mystery, and atmospheric worldbuilding.

## Story premise

Andrew is an ordinary human from Earth. After discovering a mysterious portal, he is transported to Aethelgard, a radiant planet illuminated by multiple suns and shaped by a strict system of rule. The game follows his journey through unfamiliar environments and encounters.

This overview intentionally avoids major story revelations. Keep all public descriptions spoiler-free.

## Gameplay

- 2D side-scrolling platforming and movement challenges.
- Close-range combat and enemy encounters.
- Exploration of science-fiction environments.
- Character encounters and narrative progression.
- Mystery-led storytelling and worldbuilding.
