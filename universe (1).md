# Neon Aether — Universe Guide

> **A spoiler-free reference to the world of Neon Aether.**
>
> This document covers setting, places, groups, and characters without revealing major story twists, later-chapter revelations, character fates, or the ending. It is based on the developer-provided game script. Details not established in that source are intentionally left out.

## About the universe

Neon Aether is a science-fiction adventure that takes place across unusual worlds, each with its own environment, inhabitants, dangers, and systems of power.

The universe combines futuristic technology, strange planetary environments, mysterious voices, and societies with very different ideas about order and freedom. The player encounters these worlds through Andrew's journey.

This guide focuses on information that can be shared without spoiling the story.

## Aethelgard

Aethelgard is the first world encountered in the story.

### Environment and appearance

- The planet is illuminated by multiple suns.
- Its cities are described as exceptionally clean and radiant.
- Glass and white stone are prominent in its architecture.
- The landscape has a bright, polished appearance.

### Government and society

Aethelgard is ruled by **Queen Vespera**, who is associated with the planet's strict system of order.

The authorities enforce rules aggressively. Guards treat humans as a threat because they regard humanity as chaotic. The player quickly discovers that simply being human can make someone a target in Aethelgard.

### Important places

The story script names several areas associated with Aethelgard:

- **The city:** A major urban environment with guards and residential buildings.
- **The prison:** A heavily secured facility used to detain people considered threats to the established order.
- **The Mainland:** A region beyond the prison where the journey continues.
- **The Wastes:** A named region on the route across the planet.
- **The Frontier:** Another named area along the journey.
- **The mountain border:** A guarded perimeter near the approach to the Royal Palace.
- **The Royal Palace:** The seat of Queen Vespera's rule.

The script names these places but does not provide a complete geographical map or detailed descriptions of every region.

## Groups and institutions

### The royal guards

The guards enforce Aethelgard's laws and maintain the Queen's order. They pursue people they consider threats, including humans and members of the Novari.

### The Novari

The Novari are described as a clan of assassins. They are hunted by the guards and are associated with resistance against Queen Vespera.

The script introduces **Nyx** as a Novari assassin who rebels against the Queen.

## Characters

The following descriptions are limited to information that can be shared without revealing major story developments.

### Andrew

Andrew is the human protagonist. His journey begins when he discovers a portal in his home and arrives in Aethelgard.

He is curious, outspoken, and often responds to unfamiliar situations with humour. His journey takes him into contact with unfamiliar worlds, people, and conflicts.

### Queen Vespera

Queen Vespera rules Aethelgard. Her government maintains strict order, and her authorities treat humans as dangerous outsiders.

### Nyx

Nyx is a member of the Novari, a clan of assassins opposed to the Queen's rule. She is resourceful, irreverent, and willing to take risks.

### Valtor

Valtor is a high-ranking military commander who pursues the human in the name of maintaining order. He is described as stoic and methodical.

### Solan

Solan is an observant person who knows a route toward the Royal Palace. He has a personal grievance against the Queen.

### Tyro

Tyro is a telepath who can perceive other people's thoughts. He offers help to the group during their journey.

### Raya

Raya is a mountain sentry and one of the Queen's protectors. Raya patrols the high peaks and challenges those who cross the perimeter.

## Technology, abilities, and unusual phenomena

The script establishes that the universe contains technology and abilities beyond ordinary present-day human experience.

Confirmed examples include:

- **Portals:** A portal transports Andrew from his home to another world.
- **Spaceships:** Interplanetary travel is possible using advanced spacecraft.
- **Telepathy:** Some characters can perceive or communicate through thoughts.
- **Unusual objects:** Andrew is given a mission involving the collection of mysterious objects. Their full significance is part of the story and is deliberately not explained here.
- **Special equipment:** Some environments require protective equipment for survival.

This guide does not explain the deeper origin or full purpose of these phenomena.

## Themes and atmosphere

The Aethelgard setting introduces several ideas that recur in the game's science-fiction world:

- **Order and freedom:** Who gets to define order, and what happens to people who do not fit its rules?
- **Outsiders and belonging:** Different worlds have different ways of treating those who come from elsewhere.
- **Power and resistance:** Political authority, enforcement, and rebellion shape life on Aethelgard.
- **Mystery and discovery:** Andrew encounters unfamiliar places, people, and phenomena whose meaning is not always immediately clear.

The atmosphere combines bright, futuristic environments with tension, danger, humour, and mystery.
