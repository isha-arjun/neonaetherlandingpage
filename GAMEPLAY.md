# Neon Aether — Gameplay Guide

> **A spoiler-free overview of the gameplay in Neon Aether.**
>
> This page describes gameplay elements shown in the developer-provided script. It avoids later-chapter story details and does not present unconfirmed or planned features as finished. Controls and mechanics may change during development.

## What kind of game is Neon Aether?

Neon Aether is a story-driven, 2D side-scrolling science-fiction platformer. Players follow Andrew through unfamiliar environments, navigate dangerous situations, encounter hostile characters, and take part in close-range combat.

The experience combines platforming, action, exploration, character encounters, and a science-fiction story.

## Movement and platforming

Movement is a core part of navigating the game's environments. Players move through level spaces, jump across obstacles, and make their way past guards and environmental challenges.

The developer script includes the following keyboard controls in an in-game instruction prompt:

| Action | Control shown in the script |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD |
| Jump | Space |
| Block | Shift |

These are the controls documented in the script and may be adjusted as development continues.

## Combat

The script includes close-range combat encounters. Players can block and use timed sequences of attacks.

An in-game instruction prompt describes this basic unarmed combat sequence:

- One click: punch
- Two clicks: kick
- Three clicks: power move

The prompt also describes cooldowns for kicks and power moves. This means players need to pay attention to timing rather than repeatedly using every move without pause.

Some encounters introduce a blade. In the documented blade-combat prompt, the attack sequence is:

- One click: stab
- Two clicks: slash
- Three clicks: power move

The blade sequence also includes cooldowns for certain attacks. The exact control scheme and combat balance may change during development.

## Enemies and encounters

Andrew's journey brings him into conflict with guards and other hostile opponents. The script includes both ordinary combat encounters and larger boss encounters.

Different opponents can require different approaches. The documented encounters include blocking, choosing when to attack, and learning how to deal with an opponent's strengths.

This page does not list later-chapter opponents or describe their story roles, to avoid spoilers.

## Boss encounters

Boss encounters are part of the gameplay described in the script. One documented encounter teaches the player to use a blade and pay attention to an opponent's vulnerable area.

Boss encounters are intended to feel distinct from ordinary movement through a level. Specific boss identities, later encounters, and story outcomes are intentionally not described here.

## Levels and progression

The script is structured around a sequence of levels. Early gameplay takes Andrew through Aethelgard, including areas connected to the city, the prison, and the route toward the Royal Palace.

Across these levels, the player encounters new characters, navigates different environments, and faces increasingly involved situations. This guide does not publish a complete level-by-level walkthrough because that would reveal story events and surprises.

## Exploration and story

Exploration is part of the experience: players move through unfamiliar places, discover who inhabits them, and learn about the worlds through the journey.

Gameplay and narrative are closely connected. Encounters with characters and the environments Andrew visits contribute to the unfolding science-fiction adventure.

## Gameplay at a glance

- **Genre:** 2D side-scrolling, story-driven science-fiction platformer
- **Core activities:** Platforming, jumping, navigating levels, and close-range combat
- **Combat elements documented in the script:** Blocking, punches, kicks, power moves, and blade attacks
- **Progression:** A sequence of levels with new environments and encounters
- **Story integration:** Characters and events are encountered as part of Andrew's journey
