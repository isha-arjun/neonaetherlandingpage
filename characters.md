# Neon Aether — Character Guide

> **Meet the characters of Neon Aether — spoiler-free.**
>
> This guide introduces the protagonist and key characters using information established in the developer-provided script. It avoids major plot developments, character fates, hidden identities, and later-chapter revelations.

## Andrew

**Role:** Protagonist  
**Origin:** Earth

Andrew is an ordinary human whose life changes when he discovers a mysterious portal in his home and is transported to the unfamiliar planet of Aethelgard.

He is curious, quick to react, and often uses humour when faced with strange or dangerous situations. In Aethelgard, being human immediately puts him at odds with the authorities, and he must find his way through an unfamiliar world.

**What defines him:**
- Curious about the worlds and people he encounters.
- Outspoken, with a tendency to respond with humour.
- Thrown into situations far beyond his everyday experience.

Andrew is at the centre of Neon Aether's science-fiction adventure. His journey introduces players to the universe one discovery at a time.

## Nyx

**Role:** Novari assassin and rebel  
**Affiliation:** The Novari

Nyx is the first major ally Andrew meets on Aethelgard. She belongs to the Novari, a clan of assassins hunted by the planet's guards.

Nyx is sharp, irreverent, confident, and comfortable taking risks. Her humour often appears even when a situation is dangerous. She knows how to survive in Aethelgard and helps Andrew understand the danger surrounding him.

**What defines her:**
- A member of the hunted Novari clan.
- Resourceful and familiar with life under Aethelgard's authorities.
- Witty, bold, and willing to challenge the established order.

Nyx brings energy, experience, and a rebellious perspective to Andrew's journey.

## Valtor

**Role:** High-ranking military commander  
**Affiliation:** Aethelgard's forces

Valtor is a high-ranking commander tasked with pursuing Andrew. He is stoic, disciplined, and methodical, approaching his duty with cold precision.

He represents the military authority and strict enforcement of order that Andrew encounters on Aethelgard.

**What defines him:**
- A senior figure in Aethelgard's military.
- Serious, controlled, and duty-driven.
- A formidable presence in Andrew's early journey.

## Solan

**Role:** Guide and ally

Solan is an observant person Andrew and Nyx encounter while travelling toward the Royal Palace. He is good at reading people and noticing details others might miss.

Solan knows the route ahead and offers to accompany the group. He is direct about his views and has personal reasons for opposing Queen Vespera's rule.

**What defines him:**
- Highly observant and perceptive.
- Familiar with the route toward the Royal Palace.
- Direct, determined, and motivated by his opposition to the Queen.

## Tyro

**Role:** Telepath and ally

Tyro is a telepath who can perceive other people's thoughts. Andrew encounters him in a small town, where Tyro demonstrates his ability and offers help.

His telepathic abilities make him a valuable source of information when the group needs to understand what is happening around them.

**What defines him:**
- Has telepathic abilities.
- Helps the group navigate the dangers around them.
- Speaks confidently and gets straight to the point.

## Queen Vespera

**Role:** Ruler of Aethelgard

Queen Vespera rules the planet Aethelgard. Her government maintains a strict system of order, enforced by guards and military commanders.

Her authority shapes the society Andrew encounters, including the way humans and the Novari are treated.

**What defines her:**
- The ruler of Aethelgard.
- The central authority behind the planet's system of order.
- A powerful figure whose rule affects the lives of the people on the planet.

## Raya

**Role:** Mountain sentry and royal protector  
**Affiliation:** Queen Vespera's protectors

Raya patrols the high mountain region near the Royal Palace. Raya is identified as the Queen's second protector and is responsible for stopping people who cross the guarded perimeter.

The script describes Raya as nimble and capable in combat.

**What defines Raya:**
- Patrols the high mountain border.
- Serves as the Queen's second protector.
- Guards the approach to the Royal Palace.

## Character relationships at a glance

- **Andrew and Nyx:** The first major partnership in Andrew's journey through Aethelgard.
- **Andrew and Valtor:** A human outsider comes into conflict with a commander enforcing Aethelgard's rules.
- **Andrew, Nyx, and Solan:** Solan joins their journey and helps guide them toward the Royal Palace.
- **Andrew, Nyx, Solan, and Tyro:** Tyro offers his telepathic abilities to help the group.
- **Queen Vespera and her protectors:** Vespera's authority is enforced by the planet's guards, military, and royal protectors.
